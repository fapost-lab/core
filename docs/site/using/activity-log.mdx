---
title: "Activity log"
description: "Who did what in the panels, and when."
---

The activity log lists what people did in this tenant's admin panel, assistant
console and flow builder: who acted, what they did, to what, and when — newest
first, and read only. It is under **System** in the admin panel's menu.

## What is recorded

| Area | Actions |
|---|---|
| **Assistants** | Created, changed, deleted; assistant settings saved |
| **Channels** | Created, changed, deleted; token replaced; webhook hash rotated |
| **Flows** | Created, changed, deleted, published (with the version); flow groups created, renamed, deleted |
| **Broadcasts** | Created, changed, deleted, sent, cancelled |
| **Contacts** | Tags and groups changed on a contact's card; contact groups and segments created, changed, deleted |
| **Staff** | Users invited, changed, given other roles, activated, deactivated, deleted, sent a new activation link; roles created, changed, deleted |
| **Settings** | Tenant settings saved; workspace deletion requested or cancelled |
| **Sign-in** | A staff user signed in or signed out |

What runs on its own is not recorded: flow sessions, message delivery, webhooks,
a broadcast reaching its contacts, and the builder saving a draft while you edit
(publishing is recorded).

## What an entry shows

**Details** on a row opens what changed. Names, statuses, switches, languages and
role permissions are shown with their old and new values. Texts — assistant
messages, broadcast messages, a segment's rules, a channel's settings, a password —
show only that they changed. Tokens, webhook hashes and passwords never appear in
the log in any form. For a contact the log names neither the person nor their
details: the entry shows a short id and which part of the card changed.

## Who acted

Each entry names its author as they were named at the time, so it stays readable
after the user is renamed or deleted.

| Shown as | Means |
|---|---|
| A user's name | A staff user acting in the panels |
| **Platform support** (operator) | The platform's support team inside your tenant; the entry names the operator and links to their visit in [Support access](/using/users#platform-support) |
| **System** | A command or scheduled task, not a person |

## Finding something

Search matches the author and the name of what was changed. Filters narrow the
list by area, author (a user, platform support or the system), assistant and
period: the last 7, 30 (the default) or 90 days, or everything kept.

## Who sees it

The **View the activity log** permission opens it; administrators have it. It
shows what every colleague did, so the role editor marks it sensitive — see
[Roles and permissions](/using/roles-and-permissions).

## How long entries are kept

A year, unless the installation sets otherwise: entries older than the platform
setting `audit.retention_days` are deleted every night by `audit:prune`
([Platform settings](/self-hosting/platform-settings)). `0` keeps them forever.
